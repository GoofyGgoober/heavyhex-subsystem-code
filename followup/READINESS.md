# Kingston circuit and analysis checks

Checks recorded for the 14 October 2026 experiments. Hardware outcomes and reproduction
commands are in the [follow-up README](README.md).

## Circuits and decoders

- With two twirls per circuit, Stim's signed flows match the intended logical circuit.
  Without noise, detectors do not fire and the logical outcome is correct. Idle-test
  data return their prepared value in the measurement basis.
- The Z idle test uses the X test's template wire for wire, including data qubits.
  T1 and T2 circuits read 1 without noise; their waits contain no pulse except the
  Hahn echo's X.
- Circuit distance is d in both memories at distances 3 and 5, checked at rounds 2 and 4.
  Matching-graph logical paths have 3 and 5 edges in X memory, but 3 and 3 in Z memory.
- Uniform matching weights correct all single faults. At d=3, cap 0.1 miscorrects
  2, 3, 5 and 7 fault classes in X memory at rounds 1–4, and 1, 1, 2 and 3 in Z memory.
  Cap 0.05 also miscorrects single faults. The README explains the trade-off with
  total model error.

## Simulated-data recovery

These checks use data generated by the assumed model; they test the analysis pipeline,
not the model's adequacy for the device.

- Exp 1 recovers T_q on the 25 data qubits with pulls −0.18 ± 0.95. Fitted ε and Λ
  are within one standard error of the planted values; O/M is 0.97–1.04.
- The 1024-shot lifetime simulation recovers 1/T1 and 1/T2 over the 65 qubits with
  pulls −0.19 ± 0.73 and −0.07 ± 0.88. One of the 130 estimates is beyond two
  standard errors. In-situ decay per round has pulls −0.06 ± 0.90 in Z and
  +0.23 ± 0.88 in X over the 25 data qubits.
- Using both simulated Exp 1 jobs, predictions at the fitted in-situ T1 and T_q
  differ by less than 3% from predictions at the planted values; O/M is 0.94–1.05.
  This check used the 1024-shot lifetime design, not the final 512-shot life-t job.

## Measurement limits

Twirling and XY4 constrain the idle test to the sum of upward and downward transition
rates, not their separate physical values. Joint in-situ T1 and T_q estimates are
strongly anticorrelated; the decay per round is better determined than either lifetime.

The simultaneous Hahn echoes flip neighbouring qubits together, so they do not refocus
their ZZ coupling. The measured Hahn T2 is therefore not interchangeable with IBM's
reported T2. Neither a two-state fit nor readout clouds without a calibrated |2>
reference identify leakage. The paper retains these limitations.
